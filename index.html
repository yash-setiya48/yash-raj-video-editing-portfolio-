QUESTIONS

FAQ

What's the typical turnaround time?

Most short-form edits are delivered in 48–72 hours. Long-form or complex projects take longer and are quoted upfront.

How many revisions are included?

Depends on the package — Basic includes 1 round, Standard includes 2, and Premium includes unlimited revisions.

What file formats do you deliver?

MP4 by default, exported in whatever resolution and aspect ratio your platform needs (16:9, 9:16, 1:1).

How do I send my raw footage?

Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer links work fine — I'll share an upload link once we lock in the project.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to start, remaining 50% on final delivery. Bigger projects can be split into milestones.
